Energy harvesting · Open hardware prototype

Self-Powered IV Curve Tester
with Single DSSC

Designed to harvest energy from one dye-sensitized solar cell, store it, then measure that same cell using a 16-step load bank, ADS122C04 and ESP32-S3. E-paper retains the result without continuous refresh.

How can 0.7 V charge it?

U7, AEM10941, boosts the voltage into three parallel 1.5 F supercapacitors: 4.5 F total. Its specified cold-start point is about 0.38 V / 3 µW. This board targets 70% of open-circuit voltage for MPPT.

At a simultaneous loaded operating point, 0.7 V × 30 mA = 21 mW. Energy accumulates with the main electronics off; U8, TPS61023, then supplies 5 V for short operating bursts.

DSSC → K1 → energy harvester → supercapacitors → 5 V boost → controller / relay

If 0.7 V and 30 mA are Voc and Isc, they cannot be multiplied as usable power: an assumed 0.5–0.7 fill factor gives roughly 1–2 hours from empty. Calculations and assumptions.

What does K1 do?

  • Coil off: connects the cell to the harvester, with no coil holding power.
  • Coil on: connects the cell to the test loads, separating it from the harvester input during the sweep.

The non-latching 5 V relay draws about 21 mA / 0.105 W while measuring, from stored energy. Ground remains shared.
